Nevada Licensing at a Glance

Nevada gives a permit at 15 and a half and a license at 16 after driver ed. The 50-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Nevada Different

Nevada's manual stresses desert heat, limited-water-distance planning, and Las Vegas strip congestion plus mountain winter driving.

The Written Test on This Page
