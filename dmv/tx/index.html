Texas Licensing at a Glance

Texas requires a 6-hour adult driver ed course and issues a permit at 15. The 30-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Texas Different

Teen drivers must complete 32 classroom hours before the road exam, and the manual adds hurricane evacuation, ranch roads, and rural merging.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 54 practice questions follow the topics in the official Texas driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Texas Test-Day Tip
