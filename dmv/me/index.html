Maine Licensing at a Glance

Maine issues a permit at 15 and a license at 16 and a half after driver ed. The 50-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Maine Different

Maine's handbook stresses moose crossings, coastal fog, and narrow rural roads with limited sightlines.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 50 practice questions follow the topics in the official Maine driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Verified Maine licensing and road-rule facts

These Maine-specific notes link back to the agency or handbook page used to verify them. Official requirements can change, so follow the linked source when preparing an application or test appointment.

  • Maine's minimum learner's permit age is 15; applicants under 18 must complete approved driver education. Official source ↗
  • Applicants under 21 must hold the permit for six months and complete at least 70 driving hours, including 10 at night, before the road test. Official source ↗
