Iowa Licensing at a Glance

Iowa issues a learner permit at 14 and a license at 17 after driver ed and supervised hours. The 35-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Iowa Different

Iowa's manual highlights rural intersection safety, farm-equipment sharing, and winter black-ice across its agricultural network.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 25 practice questions follow the topics in the official Iowa driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Verified Iowa licensing and road-rule facts

  • Iowa uses a 0.08 OWI threshold for most drivers and a 0.02 threshold for drivers under 21. Official source ↗
