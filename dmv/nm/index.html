New Mexico Licensing at a Glance

New Mexico issues a permit at 15 and a license at 16 and a half after driver ed. The 25-question test and road test are required.

What Makes New Mexico Different

New Mexico's handbook covers high-desert glare, open-range livestock, and tribal-land road rules unique to the region.
