Florida Licensing at a Glance

Florida requires the TLSAE drug-and-alcohol course before the learner permit at 15. The 50-question test is longer than average.

What Makes Florida Different

After the permit, a road test follows. The manual stresses hurricane-evacuation routes, tourist intersections, and senior renewal.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 50 practice questions follow the topics in the official Florida driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Florida Test-Day Tip

Florida candidates must remember TLSAE content and hurricane-evacuation signs, which appear on the longer test.

Most-Missed Florida Topic

The frequent Florida miss is the hurricane-evacuation route sign color, tested specifically on the longer exam.

Preparing for the FL Exam

Finish the TLSAE course first, then study hurricane-evacuation sign colors, which the longer 50-question Florida test includes by specific color and shape.

On the Day in FL

On exam day, stay calm through the longer format, yield to tourists at heavy intersections, and trust the TLSAE content you already completed.

After You Pass in FL

After the TLSAE and permit, book the road test; Florida re-checks hurricane-evacuation awareness on the road.

Keep the permit for practice and respect the learner limits until the full license is issued.
