Georgia Licensing at a Glance

Georgia issues a learner permit at 15 and a provisional at 16 after Joshua's Law driver education. The 40-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Georgia Different

Joshua's Law sets a 30-hour classroom and 6-hour behind-the-wheel minimum, and the manual adds Atlanta express-lane sections.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 50 practice questions follow the topics in the official Georgia driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Georgia Test-Day Tip

Georgia applicants should study Joshua's Law hour requirements and Atlanta express-lane rules, common miss areas.

Most-Missed Georgia Topic

Georgia testers often miss the express-lane entry rule, where the manual is specific and the exam tests the exact condition.
