Kentucky Licensing at a Glance

Kentucky gives a permit at 16 and a license at 16 and a half after a graduate driver program. The 40-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Kentucky Different

Kentucky's manual stresses Appalachian curves, horse-country shared roads, and winter icing on bridges.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 50 practice questions follow the topics in the official Kentucky driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Kentucky Test-Day Tip

Kentucky applicants should study Appalachian-curve speed and bridge icing, the most-tested safety topics.

Most-Missed Kentucky Topic

The Kentucky miss is bridge-icing vs road-icing; the manual distinguishes them and the exam tests the difference.

Preparing for the KY Exam

Study Appalachian-curve speed and bridge icing, then learn the bridge-icing vs road-icing distinction Kentucky tests specifically on the 40-question exam.

On the Day in KY

Slow on horse-country shared roads, yield on narrow mountain curves, and brake early on iced bridge spans on test day.

After You Pass in KY

After the permit, finish the graduate program and book the road test; Kentucky re-checks Appalachian and bridge-icing habits behind the wheel.

Slow on horse-country roads and carry the permit on every practice drive until the license comes.
