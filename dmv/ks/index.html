Kansas Licensing at a Glance

Kansas issues a permit at 14 and a license at 16 and a half after driver ed. The 25-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Kansas Different

Kansas' handbook adds tornado-season visibility, farm-vehicle sharing, and small-town intersection rules common across the plains.
