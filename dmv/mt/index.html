Montana Licensing at a Glance

Montana issues a permit at 14 and a half and a license at 16. The 33-question test and road test are required, with supervised hours logged.

What Makes Montana Different

Montana's manual stresses open-range livestock, mountain passes, and long empty-highway speed awareness.

Verified Montana licensing and road-rule facts

These Montana-specific notes link back to the agency or handbook page used to verify them. Official requirements can change, so follow the linked source when preparing an application or test appointment.

  • Montana teen drivers must complete at least 50 supervised driving hours, including 10 at night, over a minimum of six months. Official source ↗
  • A teen may obtain a non-commercial learner permit at age 16, or at age 15 after successfully completing a state-approved traffic education program. Official source ↗
  • A driver under 18 must hold the non-commercial learner permit for at least six consecutive months before applying for a first-year restricted license. Official source ↗
