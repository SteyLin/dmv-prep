Louisiana Licensing at a Glance

Louisiana issues a learner permit at 15 and a license at 17 after driver ed. The 40-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Louisiana Different

Louisiana's handbook highlights hurricane evacuation, heavy-rain hydroplaning, and long-span causeway driving.

Verified Louisiana licensing and road-rule facts

These Louisiana-specific notes link back to the agency or handbook page used to verify them. Official requirements can change, so follow the linked source when preparing an application or test appointment.

  • Louisiana's Stage 1 learner's permit has a minimum age of 15 and requires an 80% score on the knowledge test. Official source ↗
  • Before upgrading to an intermediate license, a parent must certify 50 behind-the-wheel hours, including 15 at night, and the permit must have been held for 180 days. Official source ↗
  • Louisiana's Stage 2 intermediate license generally prohibits driving from 11:00 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. unless accompanied by an adult age 21 or older. Official source ↗
