Hawaii Licensing at a Glance

Hawaii runs licensing at the county level, so rules vary slightly by island. The permit starts at 15 and a road test is required at 16.

What Makes Hawaii Different

Each county schedules its own road tests, and the manual covers narrow tropical roads, resort pedestrian zones, and volcanic visibility.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 25 practice questions follow the topics in the official Hawaii driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Hawaii Test-Day Tip

Hawaii testers should review narrow-road and pedestrian-resort rules; each county weights local conditions differently.

Most-Missed Hawaii Topic

The Hawaii miss is usually the resort-zone crosswalk rule, where pedestrians always have priority by state law.

Preparing for the HI Exam

Because each county runs its own road test, check the county-specific booking and review resort-zone crosswalk priority, where pedestrians always have the right of way.

On the Day in HI

On the day, slow on narrow coastal roads, use lights in volcanic vog, and yield to pedestrians in resort zones without hesitation.

After You Pass in HI

After the county permit, book the county road test; Hawaii re-checks resort-zone and narrow-road habits behind the wheel.

Carry the permit to practice and yield to pedestrians in resort zones until the license is issued.
