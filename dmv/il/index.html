Illinois Licensing at a Glance

Illinois issues a permit at 15 and requires 50 supervised hours, including 10 at night. The 35-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Illinois Different

Illinois mandates a 6-hour adult driver ed for some new drivers and adds Chicago expressway and rail-crossing safety.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 25 practice questions follow the topics in the official Illinois driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Illinois Test-Day Tip

Illinois applicants should review Chicago expressway merging and the 50-hour log, both referenced on the exam.

Verified Illinois licensing and road-rule facts

  • Illinois applicants age 15–17 may receive an instruction permit with proof of enrollment in an approved driver education course, and the permit must be held at least nine consecutive months before licensing. Official source ↗
  • Drivers under 18 must complete at least 50 hours of supervised driving, including 10 hours at night, before becoming eligible for a license at age 16. Official source ↗
