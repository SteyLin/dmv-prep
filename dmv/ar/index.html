Arkansas Licensing at a Glance

Arkansas gives a learner permit at 14 and a provisional at 16. The written test is 25 questions and teens log supervised hours.

What Makes Arkansas Different

The handbook highlights school-bus stopping law and phosphate-truck sharing on highways before the road test confirms control.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 52 practice questions follow the topics in the official Arkansas driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Arkansas Test-Day Tip

In Arkansas, learners lose points on school-bus stopping, so drill that section and the four-way-stop yield order.

Most-Missed Arkansas Topic

The usual Arkansas miss is the school-bus stopping distance on a two-lane road, a rule the exam tests with specific footage numbers.

How to Prepare for Arkansas

  • The school-bus stopping distance rule

Key Arkansas Road Rules

  • Stopping for buses on two-lane roads by law
  • Yielding at four-way stops in order of arrival
  • Sharing highways with phosphate trucks
