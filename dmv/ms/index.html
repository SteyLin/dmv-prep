Mississippi Licensing at a Glance

Mississippi gives a permit at 15 and a license at 16 after driver ed. The 30-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Mississippi Different

Mississippi's handbook highlights hurricane evacuation, heavy-rain hydroplaning, and small-town intersection right-of-way.
