North Carolina Licensing at a Glance

North Carolina gives a permit at 15 and a license at 16 after driver ed. The 25-question test and road test are required.

What Makes North Carolina Different

North Carolina uses a graduated provisional stage and adds hurricane evacuation, research-triangle traffic, and deer-crossing.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 50 practice questions follow the topics in the official North Carolina driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

North Carolina Test-Day Tip

North Carolina applicants should review hurricane evacuation and deer-crossing rules, often tested in the state.

Most-Missed North Carolina Topic
