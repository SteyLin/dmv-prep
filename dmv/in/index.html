Indiana Licensing at a Glance

Indiana gives a learner permit at 15 and a license at 16 and a half after driver ed. The 50-question exam and road test are required.

What Makes Indiana Different

Indiana logs supervised hours and highlights rural-intersection law, school zones, and lake-effect snow routes.

Verified Indiana licensing and road-rule facts

These Indiana-specific notes link back to the agency or handbook page used to verify them. Official requirements can change, so follow the linked source when preparing an application or test appointment.

  • An Indiana applicant age 16 or older must pass a vision screening and knowledge exam to obtain a learner's permit. Official source ↗
  • An applicant may qualify at age 15 by showing enrollment in a BMV-licensed behind-the-wheel training provider and also passing the vision and knowledge exams. Official source ↗
