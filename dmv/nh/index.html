New Hampshire Licensing at a Glance

New Hampshire issues a permit at 15 and a half and a license at 16. The 40-question test and road test are required.

What Makes New Hampshire Different

New Hampshire stresses White Mountain passes, moose crossings, and lake-region roads, with no primary belt-law contrast noted.
