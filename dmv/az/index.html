Arizona Licensing at a Glance

Arizona issues a graduated permit at 15 and a license at 16 after driver education. The written exam is 30 questions.

What Makes Arizona Different

Arizona is strict on identity at issuance and its manual gives extra space to extreme-heat driving and dust-storm safety.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 53 practice questions follow the topics in the official Arizona driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Arizona Test-Day Tip

Arizona applicants should study dust-storm and extreme-heat procedures; the 30-question exam tests them directly.

Most-Missed Arizona Topic

Arizona testers often miss the dust-storm "pull off, lights off" rule, which the manual states precisely and the exam checks by wording.
