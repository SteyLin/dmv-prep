North Dakota Licensing at a Glance

North Dakota issues a permit at 14 and a license at 16. The 25-question test and road test are required.

What Makes North Dakota Different

North Dakota's handbook covers whiteout blizzards, farm-equipment sharing, and oil-field truck traffic in the west.
