New York Licensing at a Glance

New York issues a permit at 16 and requires a 5-hour pre-licensing course before the road test. The 20-question written test and road test are required.

What Makes New York Different

New York adds a separate sign-recognition section and covers NYC congestion, taxi interactions, and roundabout yields.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 85 practice questions follow the topics in the official New York driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

New York Test-Day Tip

New York testers should drill the separate sign section and NYC congestion rules, the state's unique exam part.

Most-Missed New York Topic

The New York miss is the separate sign test, where shapes are tested without words and speed matters.
