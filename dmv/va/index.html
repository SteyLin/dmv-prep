Virginia Licensing at a Glance

Virginia gives a permit at 15 and a half and a license at 16 and a half after driver ed. The 35-question test and road test are required.

What Makes Virginia Different

Virginia adds a separate sign test section and covers D.C.-beltway merging, coastal evacuation, and Blue Ridge curves.

The Written Test on This Page

Our 58 practice questions follow the topics in the official Virginia driver handbook: road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, work zones, and sharing the road with cyclists, pedestrians, and large trucks. Drill them, then read every explanation to learn the rule, not just the answer.

Virginia Test-Day Tip

Virginia candidates should review D.C.-beltway merging and the separate sign test, common miss areas.

Most-Missed Virginia Topic

The Virginia miss is the separate sign test, where shapes are tested without words and speed matters.
