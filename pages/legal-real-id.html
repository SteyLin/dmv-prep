Real ID & Federal ID

Real ID is a federal standard for state-issued driver licenses and ID cards. After enforcement, a standard license may no longer be accepted for boarding domestic flights or entering federal facilities. A Real ID-compliant card, or another federally accepted document, is required.

Documents You Need

  • Proof of identity, such as a birth certificate or valid passport
  • Proof of your Social Security number
  • Two proofs of state residency, like a lease and a utility bill
  • Name-change documents if your current name differs

How to Spot a Real ID

Look for a star mark in the upper-right corner of the card. Standard IDs without the star are not REAL ID compliant and will not work for federal purposes once enforcement is fully in effect.

Accepted Alternatives

A U.S. passport, passport card, or military ID are accepted federal alternatives to a Real ID for domestic air travel. If you already carry one, you do not strictly need a Real ID for flying.

Official: dhs.gov/real-id

Tip: Apply early. Many DMV offices require appointments and document checks can take time.

What REAL ID Is

REAL ID is a federal standard for state-issued licenses and ID cards. After the enforcement date, only a REAL ID (or a passport) is accepted for boarding domestic flights and entering federal facilities. A standard license no longer works for those purposes.

Documents You Need

  • Proof of identity (birth certificate or passport)
  • Social Security number evidence
  • Two proofs of state residency (mail with your address)
  • Name-change documents if your name differs

Getting One

Visit the DMV in person with original documents — copies are rejected. There is usually no extra fee beyond a standard renewal, but you must plan a trip since online renewal does not issue a first REAL ID. A star marking on the card confirms compliance.

If You Only Have a Standard License

You can still drive, vote, and buy alcohol with a standard license. REAL ID only changes federal access — flights and federal buildings. A valid passport works everywhere REAL ID is required, so travelers without REAL ID are not stranded if they carry a passport.

Common Delays

Name mismatches are the top reason for rejection. If you changed your name through marriage or court order, bring the document linking the old and new names or the DMV will turn you away.
