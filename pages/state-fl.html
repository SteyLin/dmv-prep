Florida Driver License Guide

Florida licenses are handled by the Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (DHSMV), served locally through county tax collector offices. The standard non-commercial license is a Class E license.

Class E License Steps

New drivers first complete the TLSAE (Traffic Law and Substance Abuse Education) course, then pass the Class E knowledge exam and vision test to receive a learner's license. After supervised practice, they take the road test for a full Class E license.

Learner License Rules

  • Daytime driving only for the first three months, then until 10 p.m. afterward
  • Always accompanied by a licensed driver 21 or older in the front seat
  • Held for at least 12 months before an unrestricted license for teens

Road Test

The test covers parking, turns, stopping, and following distance. Bring a vehicle with valid registration, insurance, and working equipment. Many offices require an appointment.

Renewal and New Residents

Florida licenses are renewed online or in office. New residents must obtain a Florida license within 30 days of establishing residency and surrender their out-of-state license.

Official: flhsmv.gov

Tip: Florida requires proof of U.S. citizenship or lawful status for a REAL ID compliant license.

About the Florida Test

Florida requires a learner's permit knowledge exam covering road rules and traffic signs. The state also requires a drug and alcohol course (TLSAE) before the permit for first-time drivers, so plan that step early.

What to Study

  • Right-of-way and intersection rules
  • Speed limits in cities, school, and work zones
  • Sharing the road with motorcycles and trucks
  • Florida's specific sign shapes and meanings

Testing in Florida

The permit exam can be taken online through an approved provider or at a tax collector office. Use our practice questions to find weak spots, then re-read the handbook chapters that gave you trouble. This site is an independent study aid.

Permit to License Path

Florida issues a learner permit after the exam, then a license after the road test and holding period. Teens follow a graduated sequence with supervised hours; the TLSAE course is required before the permit for first-timers.

Florida-Specific Rules

  • Move over for stopped emergency vehicles
  • Headlights required in rain
  • Strict penalties for racing and street takeovers
