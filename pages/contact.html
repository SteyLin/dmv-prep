Before You Email

Most questions are answered in our FAQ and the region guides. If you are reporting a content error, include the exact state or country and the question wording so we can find and fix it quickly.

Response Time

We aim to reply within a few business days. High-volume periods may take longer, but every message is read. Privacy requests under GDPR or CCPA are prioritized and handled according to the applicable law.

What We Can and Cannot Do

  • We can correct practice content and clarify explanations.
  • We cannot issue licenses, book DMV appointments, or answer official exam questions.
  • For official matters, contact your licensing authority directly.

Press and Partnerships

For collaboration or media requests, use the general address above with "Partnership" in the subject line. We review proposals that align with free, accessible driver education.

Mailing a Correction

The fastest way to fix a wrong answer is a short email with the exact wording and the region. Screenshots help but are not required. We verify against the relevant handbook before updating, so accuracy comes before speed.

Feedback Welcome

Tell us which tests helped, which felt too hard, and what guide you wish existed. We read it all and use it to decide what to build next. A one-line note can shape the next update.

Emergency Note

This is an educational site, not a roadside or legal service. If you are in an urgent situation — a crash, a citation, or a licensing deadline — contact the proper authority or professional directly.

What to Expect

After you email, you will receive a human reply — not an auto-responder promising a bot. We keep answers plain and specific, and we will tell you when a fix ships if your note led to one.

Right to Be Forgotten

If you have emailed us and later want that correspondence deleted, say so and we will remove it from our inbox and any working notes, subject only to legal retention that may rarely apply.

Thank You

Reader messages are the reason the site keeps improving. Whether it is a typo or a brand-new region request, it is welcome — and it is read by a real person who cares about the answer being right.
