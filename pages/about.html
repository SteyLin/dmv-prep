About DriveReady Hub

DriveReady Hub is a free study companion for people preparing for driving knowledge exams — learner permits, driver's licenses, and theory tests across the U.S., U.K., and Canada.

Our Goal

We believe quality practice material should be free and accessible. Our tests are built to help learners check their understanding, review the "why" behind each answer, and walk into the exam more confident.

How Our Content Is Made

Questions and explanations are written as original practice material inspired by publicly available driver-handbook topics (which are in the public domain). We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any DMV, DVSA, provincial ministry, or government agency. Always confirm rules with your official licensing authority.

Independent &Supported by Ads

This site is independently operated. To keep it free, we display advertising through Google AdSense. Advertisers are chosen by Google; we do not sell or share your personal information.

What You'll Find Here

Our library includes state-by-state practice for U.S. permit and license exams, DVSA-style theory material for the U.K., and provincial practice for Canada, plus Germany, Ireland, and Australia. Beyond tests, we publish plain-language guides on buying, insuring, maintaining, and driving safely.

How to Use the Site

  • Pick your region and open a practice test.
  • Answer each question, then read the explanation — the "why" is where learning sticks.
  • Review the guides to fill gaps the test reveals.
  • Confirm anything uncertain against your official handbook.

Editorial Standards

We write original explanations and keep them current with published handbook topics. We do not scrape or resell exam content, and we clearly label this site as an independent study aid. If a rule changes in your region, always trust the official source over any practice material.

Accuracy and Corrections

Driving rules change, and handbooks differ by jurisdiction. If you spot an error or an outdated fact, email us with the state or country and the question text. We review corrections and update the material so other learners are not misled.

Open and Free

We keep the core practice and guides free for everyone. Advertising supports the hosting and the time spent maintaining content, and we never put answers or tests behind a paywall.

Our Commitment

We are a small, independent project focused on one thing: helping people pass their driving exams with confidence. There is no upsell, no premium tier, and no data harvesting buried in the fine print. What you see is the product.

How We Pay for This

Ad revenue covers servers and maintenance. We choose non-intrusive placements and let Google handle advertiser selection. If an ad ever feels off, you can use your browser's controls to limit personalized ads at any time.

Stay in Touch

We add new regions and refresh guides as rules change. Suggestions from learners shape what we build next, so reach out through the contact page with ideas or corrections — the site gets better because readers speak up.
