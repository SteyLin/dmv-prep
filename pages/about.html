About DriveReady Hub

DriveReady Hub is a free study companion for people preparing for driving knowledge exams — learner permits, driver's licenses, and theory tests across the U.S., U.K., and Canada.

Our Goal

We believe quality practice material should be free and accessible. Our tests are built to help learners check their understanding, review the "why" behind each answer, and walk into the exam more confident.

How Our Content Is Made

Questions and explanations are written as original practice material inspired by publicly available driver-handbook topics (which are in the public domain). We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any DMV, DVSA, provincial ministry, or government agency. Always confirm rules with your official licensing authority.

Independent &Supported by Ads

This site is independently operated. To keep it free, we display advertising through Google AdSense. Advertisers are chosen by Google; we do not sell or share your personal information.
