New York Driver License Guide

New York State DMV issues licenses through local DMV offices. The process includes a written test for a learner permit, a mandatory pre-licensing course, and a road test.

Learner Permit

Applicants pass a written knowledge test and vision screening to receive a learner permit. Permit holders under 18 are restricted to supervised driving and must hold the permit for at least six months.

5-Hour Pre-Licensing Course

Before the road test, every new driver must complete the state-approved 5-hour course (or a full driver education program). The certificate is required to schedule the road test.

Junior License Rules
  • Junior drivers may drive only with a supervising adult unless within the allowed area
  • Passenger and nighttime restrictions apply until age 18
  • Upstate and NYC area rules differ for early driving

Road Test and Renewal

The road test checks control, signaling, and traffic law compliance. Licenses are renewed online or in office; REAL ID requires extra documents proving identity and residency.

Official: dmv.ny.gov

Tip: NYC road tests are often booked weeks ahead — schedule early and practice in the test zone.

About the New York Test

New York's permit test is based on the state driver manual and includes a separate road-sign recognition section. A learner permit is the first step before supervised driving and the road test.

What to Study

  • Traffic signs and pavement markings
  • Right-of-way and passing rules
  • Speed limits and safe following distance
  • Defensive-driving basics

Testing in New York

The written test is given at DMV offices, often by appointment. Practice with our questions to build confidence, then focus study on the chapters where you miss answers. Our content is a study aid and not affiliated with the DMV.

Permit to License Path

New York issues a learner permit after the written test, then a road test after practice. The state enforces a junior license phase for teens with night and passenger limits until full licensure.

New York-Specific Rules

  • Right turn on red only where permitted by sign
  • Strict cell-phone ban, even hands-free for learners
  • Slow down, move over for stopped vehicles
