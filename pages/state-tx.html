Texas Driver License Guide

Texas driver licenses are issued by the Department of Public Safety (DPS), not the DMV. Whether you are a teen applying for your first permit or a new resident transferring an out-of-state license, the steps below cover what you need before visiting a DPS office.

Required Documents

Bring proof of identity (such as a birth certificate or passport), your Social Security number, and two documents proving Texas residency (lease, utility bill, or bank statement). All documents must be original or certified copies.

Learner License and Teen Drivers

Teens can apply for a learner license at 15 with driver education. At 16, after holding the learner license for at least six months and completing the Impact Texas Teen Drivers (ITTD) course, they may take the road test for a provisional license.

Impact Texas Drivers Courses

  • ITTD — required for drivers under 18 before the road test
  • ITAD — adult drivers education impact course
  • ITYR — young rural drivers program

Road Test and Renewal

The road test checks basic control, parking, and following traffic law. Licenses are renewed online through the DPS "Drive OK" portal when eligible, or in person at an office. Commercial (CDL) classes follow federal and state skill standards.

New Residents

If you move to Texas, you must transfer your out-of-state license within 90 days. Bring your current license, proofs of identity and residency, and be ready for a vision exam.

Official: Texas DPS scheduler and dps.texas.gov

Tip: Most Texas DPS services require scheduling online before visiting a license office — walk-ins are limited.

About the Texas Test

Texas issues a learner license after the knowledge exam, which is drawn from the state handbook. First-time drivers under 25 must also complete a state-approved driver education course before the road test.

What to Study

  • Signs, signals, and pavement markings
  • Right-of-way and turning rules
  • Speed limits and school bus stopping
  • Sharing the road with large vehicles

Testing in Texas

The exam is taken at a DPS office, usually by appointment. Use our practice set to find gaps, then review those handbook sections closely. This material is an independent study aid and not affiliated with the state.
