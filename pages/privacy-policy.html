Privacy Policy

DriveReady Hub ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to provide free driver-knowledge practice tests and guides. This page explains what data we handle and how.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require accounts, logins, or personal identification to use the practice tests. Quiz answers and scores are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Cookies & Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. AdSense and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. This is known as interest-based advertising.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses advertising cookies to serve ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings, or broadly via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out or YourAdChoices.

Under the EU User Consent Policy and GDPR/CCPA, where required, ad and analytics cookies are shown only after consent. You can manage or withdraw consent through the cookie banner presented on your first visit.

3. Log Data

Like most sites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard server logs (such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate location) for security and analytics. These logs are not used to identify you personally.

4. External Links

Our pages link to official government and licensing agencies for reference. We are not affiliated with any DMV, DVSA, provincial ministry, or government body. External sites have their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our content is educational and may be used by minors, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents may contact us with any concern.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your region, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at privacy@drivereadyhub.com.

1. Overview

This policy explains what information DriveReady Hub handles and how it is used. We aim for minimal collection: the site is a study aid, and you can use the practice tests without creating an account or submitting personal data.

2. Advertising and Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits and general interests. You can manage ad personalization through Google's Ad Settings and control cookies via your browser.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require registration, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data through the site. Practice answers and quiz activity stay in your browser and are not transmitted to us unless you choose to email us about them.

Types of Data and Legal Basis

Where analytics are used, they rely on legitimate interest in operating and improving the site. Where advertising cookies are set, they are disclosed here and can be refused through your browser or Google's controls. We do not sell personal information for money.

Data Retention

Server logs, if retained, are kept only as long as needed for security and operation, then deleted or aggregated. Emails you send are kept only as long as necessary to respond and resolve the request.

International Users

If you access the site from outside the United States, note that data may be processed where our hosting and ad providers operate. By using the site you consent to processing as described in this policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes means acceptance of the revised policy.
