Out-of-State License Transfer

When you move to a new state, you are generally required to exchange your old license for a local one within a set window. The exact deadline varies, but 30 to 90 days is common.

Establishing Residency

States define residency through actions like signing a lease, registering to vote, or titleing a vehicle locally. Once you meet the threshold, the clock starts on transferring your license.

What You Need

  • Your valid out-of-state license
  • Proof of identity and Social Security number
  • Two proofs of new-state residency
  • Payment for the transfer and card fee

Tests and Vision

Many states waive the written and road tests if your out-of-state license is valid, but all require a vision screening. Some states still demand the knowledge exam for certain drivers. Surrender your old license when the new one is issued.

Tip: Book the appointment right after you move — deadlines arrive faster than expected.

When You Must Switch

After establishing residency in a new state — usually within 30 to 90 days — you must exchange your old license for a local one. Keeping an out-of-state license past the deadline can bring a citation.

What Changes

  • You may need to pass a written or vision test
  • Your old license is voided once the new one is issued
  • Vehicle registration and title usually move too

Military and Students

Active-duty military and full-time students are often exempt from the deadline. Check your new state's rules so you do not accidentally let required documents lapse while exempt.

Vision and Knowledge Tests

Some states waive the road test for a clean out-of-state license but still require a written exam on local rules — roundabouts, right-on-red, and school-bus stopping distances differ by state. Study the new handbook before the appointment.

Timing the Switch

Do it early in the window, not on the last day. DMV appointments fill up, and a lapsed deadline turns a simple exchange into a citation. Bring your old license even though it will be voided.

Concurrent Registrations

Once you switch your license, also move your vehicle registration and title within the state window. Driving a car registered to your old address past the deadline can draw a separate citation even with a valid new license.
